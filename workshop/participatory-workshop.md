# Running a participatory workshop

The guide can be used to run a community workshop that explores what a local woodland offers and what could improve it. The five steps below follow the guide's appendix.

## Step 1. Understand the benefits

Briefly explain what communities can gain from time in woodlands. Invite participants to share their own and their community's experiences.

## Step 2. Introduce the 19 benefits

Hand out the guide or `../framework/community-benefits.csv` so everyone has shared reference points.

## Step 3. Reflect in small groups

Give each small group one part of the framework (or a few benefits) to focus on. Groups look at the benefits their woodland provides and the constraints that hold it back. `benefit-reflection-sheet.csv` can be used to record this.

## Step 4. Share and discuss

Each group presents its benefits, challenges and ideas. The facilitator draws out common themes and different perspectives.

## Step 5. Plan actions

Each participant shares one key takeaway. Agree who will do what, set dates and decide how people will stay in touch afterwards. Collect feedback to improve the next workshop.

## Practical tips

- Mix people from different backgrounds in each group, since priorities often differ.
- Expect some disagreement. Open communication and listening to other views help build trust.
- Offer a virtual tour or photos for people who cannot visit the woodland in person.
