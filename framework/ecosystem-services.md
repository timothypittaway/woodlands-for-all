# Ecosystem services

The guide treats ecosystem services as the foundation for the community benefits a woodland can deliver. It uses four standard types.

| Type | What it means | Woodland examples |
|---|---|---|
| Supporting | The processes all other services depend on | Photosynthesis, nutrient cycling, pollination |
| Provisioning | Things that can be harvested | Nuts, berries, mushrooms, wood fuel |
| Regulating | Processes that keep systems stable | Filtering water, regulating climate, cleaning air |
| Cultural | Non-material benefits to health and wellbeing | Walking, recreation, spiritual and aesthetic value |

A woodland's capacity to provide community benefits depends on how well these services are functioning. Woodlands differ a great deal in this.
