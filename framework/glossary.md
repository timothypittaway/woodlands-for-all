# Glossary

From *Woodlands for All* (2025).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Aesthetic appreciation | Recognising the beauty of natural landscapes, which supports enjoyment and wellbeing |
| Biodiversity | The variety of plant and animal life in a habitat, vital for ecosystem health and resilience |
| Coppicing | Cutting trees to ground level at intervals so they regrow from the base |
| Cultural services | Intangible benefits from nature, such as recreation, beauty and spiritual enrichment |
| Ecosystem services | Benefits people receive from ecosystems: provisioning, regulating, supporting and cultural |
| Ecotourism | Responsible travel to natural places that conserves them and benefits local communities, with an emphasis on interpretation and education |
| Foraging | Gathering wild food such as berries, mushrooms and herbs |
| Forest therapy | Spending time in forests for mental and physical wellbeing |
| Geolocation | Identifying a geographical position as latitude and longitude |
| Green infrastructure | A planned network of natural and semi-natural spaces that delivers ecosystem services |
| Green spaces | Natural or semi-natural areas offering ecological, social and recreational benefits |
| Inclusion | Making sure everyone feels valued and respected, whatever their background |
| Microclimate regulation | Managing the climate of a small area to create favourable conditions |
| Nature awareness | Understanding and appreciating the natural environment |
| Nature-based solutions | Actions that use natural processes to tackle challenges such as climate change and biodiversity loss |
| Participatory projects | Initiatives where community members take part in decisions and practical work |
| Provisioning services | Ecosystem services that supply products such as food, water and raw materials |
| Renewable energy | Energy from sources that replenish, such as wood from sustainably managed forests |
| Resilience | The ability of ecosystems or communities to recover from disturbance |
| Social cohesion | Connectedness and solidarity between groups |
| Spiritual wellbeing | Inner peace and a sense of connection to something greater than oneself |
| Sustainable fuel supply | Heating and energy from renewable sources such as sustainably harvested wood |
| Sustainable harvesting | Taking natural resources in a way that lets them regenerate |
| Trail camera | A discreet outdoor camera that records wildlife for monitoring |
| Woodland management | Stewardship of woodlands to keep their ecological, social and economic value |
