# Woodlands for All

Reusable materials from *Woodlands for All: A Guide Towards Fostering Community Benefits and Engagement in Communal Woodlands* (2025).

The guide is written for community groups, schools, woodland coordinators and practitioners rather than specialists. This repository turns its framework, workshop method and resource lists into files that groups can copy, adapt and use.

> Pittaway, T., Townsend, L., Hardy, C., & Marshall, A. (2025). *Woodlands for All: A Guide Towards Fostering Community Benefits and Engagement in Communal Woodlands*. James Hutton Institute. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15050621

**Read or download the full guide (PDF) on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15050621).**

## What is in this repository

| Path | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `framework/community-benefits.csv` | The 19 community benefits, grouped into 4 parts, with a short summary of each and its page in the guide |
| `framework/ecosystem-services.md` | The four ecosystem service types that underpin the benefits |
| `framework/glossary.md` | Plain-English definitions of key terms |
| `workshop/participatory-workshop.md` | The guide's five-step method for running a community workshop |
| `workshop/benefit-reflection-sheet.csv` | A blank worksheet for small groups (added in this repository) |
| `resources/organisations.csv` | UK organisations supporting woodlands, forestry and conservation |
| `resources/nbs-initiatives.csv` | European nature-based solution projects and repositories |

## The framework at a glance

| Part | Benefits |
|---|---|
| 1. Health and happiness | Connecting with nature, Relaxation, Health and wellbeing, Spiritual wellbeing, Recreation |
| 2. Environmental benefits | Microclimate regulation, Air quality, Nature accessibility, Nature awareness, Noise reduction, Aesthetic appreciation, Safe pathways in nature |
| 3. Social benefits | Sense of place, belonging and ownership, Justice, equity and inclusion, Historic and cultural landscape, Education and learning |
| 4. Economic benefits | Food provisioning, Tourism and recreation, Sustainable fuel supply |

## Background

The guide draws on a literature review of community woodland initiatives and on research with the Murray Park Trust and the community of Alford, Aberdeenshire. It was produced as part of the COEVOLVERS project.

The same benefit categories informed a wider analysis of 131 European treescape cases, published as Pittaway, Townsend and Hardy (2026) in *IJERPH*. See [nbs-treescapes-reverse-logic](https://github.com/timothypittaway/nbs-treescapes-reverse-logic).
